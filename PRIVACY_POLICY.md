# Privacy Policy for SpendWise

**Effective Date:** March 2026  
**Developer:** SaadDev ([saaddevlabs@gmail.com](mailto:saaddevlabs@gmail.com))

---

## Our Core Commitment

At **SpendWise**, we believe your financial transactions are deeply personal and belong solely to you. We have built SpendWise with a **Privacy-First** and **Offline-First** philosophy.

We do not collect, transmit, monetize, or harvest your personal or financial information under any circumstance.

---

## 1. 100% Local Device Storage
All data entered into SpendWise - including transactions, income, custom categories, monthly budgets, savings goals, and split expenses - is stored strictly on your device using a local, sandboxed SQLite database (Android Room). No database synchronization to external cloud servers takes place.

## 2. No Personal Data Collection
You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use SpendWise. The application does not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical location, or bank credentials.

## 3. Zero Telemetry and Analytics
SpendWise contains **zero analytics SDKs** (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Google Analytics). Your screen views, button clicks, and spending habits are never tracked, logged, or transmitted.

## 4. 100% Ad-Free Experience
SpendWise contains **no advertising SDKs** (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or Meta Audience Network). We do not display ads and do not share any data with advertising networks or data brokers.

## 5. Offline Operation
The application functions completely offline. It does not require an active internet connection to create, view, edit, or categorize your expenses.

## 6. Receipt Photos and Image Sandbox
Any receipt image you attach to an expense is kept exclusively in the app's private, internal file directory. Receipts are never uploaded to cloud vision services or shared with third parties.

## 7. Data Ownership and Portability
Your financial records are 100% yours:
- **CSV Export:** Export your entire transaction history to open in Excel or Google Sheets.
- **PDF Reports:** Generate formatted monthly financial summary statements.
- **Database Backup and Restore:** Create direct local SQLite database backups via Android's native file picker (Storage Access Framework).

## 8. Permanent Data Deletion
You maintain complete control over data retention:
- **Instant Wipe:** Use "Clear All Expenses" or "Clear All Categories" in Settings to permanently delete your data.
- **App Uninstallation:** Uninstalling SpendWise completely purges all local databases, cache, and receipt photos from your device.
